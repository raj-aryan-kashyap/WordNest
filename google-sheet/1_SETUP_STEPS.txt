==============================================================
 WORDNEST SYNC: SETUP STEPS
 Turn on cross-device sync and the shared word Library. Free.
==============================================================

FILES IN THIS FOLDER
  1_SETUP_STEPS.txt                 This guide (no code inside).
  2_APPS_SCRIPT_CODE.txt            The Apps Script. Open it, Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C.
  3_SPARE_SHEET_WordNest_Sync.xlsx  A ready, formatted sheet with the
                                    Library already filled. Upload to
                                    Google Drive if you need a fresh one.


--------------------------------------------------------------
 A. FIRST-TIME SETUP (about 5 minutes)
--------------------------------------------------------------

 1. Get the sheet into Google Drive.
    Upload 3_SPARE_SHEET_WordNest_Sync.xlsx to Drive. Right-click it >
    Open with > Google Sheets. Then File > Save as Google Sheets.
    Use that Google Sheets copy from now on.

 2. In the sheet: Extensions > Apps Script.

 3. Delete the sample code ("function myFunction...").

 4. Open 2_APPS_SCRIPT_CODE.txt on your computer.
    Press Ctrl+A, then Ctrl+C.
    Click in the Apps Script editor and press Ctrl+V.
    Click the Save icon. Name the project "WordNest Sync".

 5. Click Deploy > New deployment.
    Click the gear next to "Select type" and choose "Web app".
      Description:     WordNest sync
      Execute as:      Me
      Who has access:  Anyone
    Click Deploy.

 6. Click "Authorize access" and pick your Google account.
    Google may say "Google hasn't verified this app". That is normal for
    your own script: click Advanced > Go to WordNest Sync (unsafe) > Allow.

 7. Copy the "Web app" URL. It looks like:
    https://script.google.com/macros/s/AKfy...long code.../exec

 8. Check it works: open the URL in a private / incognito window.
    You should see:
    {"ok":true,"app":"WordNest","message":"Sync is running."}
    If you see a Google sign-in page instead, "Who has access" is not
    set to Anyone (see Troubleshooting).

 9. In the app folder, open js/config.js and set:
      SYNC_URL: 'paste your Web app URL here',
    Also change VERSION in js/config.js and sw.js to a new number
    (for example 1.3.0 and 'wn-1.3.0'). Upload to GitHub.

10. Reload the sheet. A "WordNest" menu appears at the top.
    Click WordNest > Add starter words to Library.
    The Library tab fills with all 366 built-in words.

11. On your phone: close and reopen the app. Go to
    Me > Sync across devices. Pick a name and a 4 to 8 number PIN.
    Tap "Turn on sync". A row appears in the Users tab.

12. On another phone: tap "I already use WordNest" on the first screen,
    and use the same name and PIN.


--------------------------------------------------------------
 B. UPDATE AN EXISTING SETUP TO A NEW SCRIPT VERSION
--------------------------------------------------------------
Use this every time you get a new 2_APPS_SCRIPT_CODE.txt.

 1. In the sheet: Extensions > Apps Script.
 2. Click in the editor, press Ctrl+A, then Delete.
 3. Open the new 2_APPS_SCRIPT_CODE.txt, Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C, then Ctrl+V
    in the editor. Save.
 4. Deploy > Manage deployments > pencil icon > Version: New version >
    Deploy. The URL stays the same, so the app keeps working.
    (Don't use "New deployment": that makes a new URL.)
 5. Reload the sheet and click WordNest > Add starter words to Library.
    It only adds words that are missing, so it is safe to click again.


--------------------------------------------------------------
 C. SMART WORD PICKS WITH GEMINI (free, about 3 minutes)
--------------------------------------------------------------
Gemini picks new words from how you learn: your goals, your level,
words you know, words you marked new, and words you got wrong.
The key stays in your sheet, so every phone gets smart picks and every
new word goes into your Library.

 1. Go to https://aistudio.google.com/apikey with your personal
    Google account. Click "Create API key". Copy it.
 2. In the sheet: WordNest > Set Gemini key. Paste the key. OK.
 3. WordNest > Test Gemini. You should see "Gemini works".
 4. Make sure the newest script is deployed (section B, step 4).

That's all. Nobody using the app ever sees a key or a setting:
  - Everyone gets smart picks, signed in or not (one shared daily cap).
  - The status icon next to the streak spins with "Finding new words"
    while Gemini works; tap it to see "3 of 25 picks used today".
  - Picked words show a small "Picked for you" tag, and a short note
    says "12 new words picked for you" when a batch arrives.
No key yet? The script menu and 'Set Gemini key' are optional: you can
also add GEMINI_API_KEY under Apps Script > Project Settings (gear) >
Script properties.

Staying inside the free limit (already built in):
  - Gemini is only asked when fewer than 10 good-fit words are left.
    Library words and starter words are used first.
  - Each call asks for 12 words.
  - At least 30 seconds between calls, at most 25 calls a day
    (300 words a day, far more than anyone learns).
  - If Google says "too many requests", the app waits 5 minutes and
    uses free dictionary words meanwhile.
  - To change the daily cap: Extensions > Apps Script > Project
    Settings (gear) > Script properties > add AI_DAILY_LIMIT, e.g. 15.
  - Words people type in themselves ("Add your own word") have their
    own cap, ADD_DAILY_LIMIT (default 40 a day). If the word is already
    in the Library it costs nothing. Without Gemini, free dictionaries
    fill in the details.
  - To change the model: add GEMINI_MODEL, e.g. gemini-2.5-flash-lite.
    You can see your real free limits at https://aistudio.google.com
    (they are per Google project and reset at midnight Pacific time).

Quality checks on every Gemini word (in the script):
  - Never a word you, or anyone in the Library, already has.
  - The past, present and future examples must really use the word.
  - Short, simple meaning; Hindi must be in Devanagari, else left out.

Note: on Google's free tier, Google may use prompts to improve its
products. The app only sends words, goals and levels, never your name,
PIN or anything personal.


--------------------------------------------------------------
 D. THE WORDNEST MENU (inside the sheet)
--------------------------------------------------------------
  Add starter words to Library   Adds any built-in words missing from
                                 the Library tab.
  Set Gemini key                 Turns on smart word picks.
  Test Gemini                    Checks the key works.
  Smart picks used today         Shows today's usage.
  Format tabs again              Restores colours, notes and filters.
  Check setup                    Shows your Web app URL.


--------------------------------------------------------------
 E. WHAT EACH TAB HOLDS
--------------------------------------------------------------
Start here
  Guide and live totals.
Users
  A to J  readable totals: learning, mastered, known, streak, last sync.
  K on    the app's full saved data as text, split into parts. Don't edit.
Words
  One row per word per person: status, box, next revision, right,
  wrong, accuracy. Rebuilt on every sync, so don't edit.
Library
  The master list of words. One row per word for everyone: context,
  word, level, meaning, how to say it, past / present / future
  examples, Hindi, source, first added date, weekday, added by, and
  who else uses it. Sorted by context, newest first.
  On every sync, each person's app gets up to 40 Library words they
  haven't seen yet, matching their goals first.
  You CAN edit meanings or Hindi here; the fix reaches anyone who
  gets that word next.
  Words people typed in themselves have Context "Added by users" and
  Source "Added by user", with their name in "Added by". The Words tab
  also has a Source column: "Added by user" or "App".


--------------------------------------------------------------
 F. TROUBLESHOOTING
--------------------------------------------------------------
"Couldn't reach the sync server"
   The phone is offline, or SYNC_URL is wrong. Open the URL in a
   browser and check you see "Sync is running".

The URL shows a Google sign-in page
   Deploy > Manage deployments > pencil > Who has access: Anyone >
   Deploy. Work or school accounts may not allow "Anyone"; use a
   personal Gmail account instead.

"That PIN is wrong for this name"
   Use the same name and PIN as on your other phone. Forgot the PIN?
   Delete your row in the Users tab, then sign in again with a new
   PIN. Your phone uploads the words it has saved.

The app still says "Sync isn't set up"
   The phone has the old version. Check step A9 (VERSION), then close
   and reopen the app.

No WordNest menu in the sheet
   Reload the sheet. The menu appears a few seconds after it opens.

The Library tab is empty or short
   Click WordNest > Add starter words to Library.

Status icon shows a red dot
   Tap it to see what went wrong (offline, sync error, or Gemini busy).
   Use "Sync now" or "Find new words now" to retry.

"Gemini did not work: The key was refused"
   Create a new key at aistudio.google.com/apikey and set it again.

Syncs feel slow with thousands of words
   In the script, set WRITE_WORDS_TAB = false, then do section B.
   Sync still works; only the Words tab stops updating.


--------------------------------------------------------------
 G. PRIVACY
--------------------------------------------------------------
Your PIN is never stored. The app sends a one-way hash of name + PIN.
Fine for a personal project, but don't reuse an important PIN.
